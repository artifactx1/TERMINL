# Arcade V1

`/os` is a focused launcher. Wen Lambo and Rekt Rumble lead; Moon Mission is the solo adventure below them. No desktop sidebar, Pixel Shop, credits, daily rewards, trophies, receipt exports, or ghost-challenge flow. The retired OS UI and receipt exporter have been removed. Legacy simulation/replay utilities remain for old data and regression coverage. Rug or Bond remains a standalone prototype at its existing URL and is not promoted in the V1 arcade.

## Longer sessions

- **Wen Lambo:** the six-course cup is the primary solo action. Quick Race and Learn to Drive remain available. Steering and online racing rules are unchanged by this refactor.
- **Rekt Rumble:** a six-fight solo circuit faces the five other fighters followed by a mirror-match finale. Each match is first to two rounds. Early opponents hesitate more; later opponents use the full existing bot policy. Health, damage and online rules are unchanged. Wins save the next opponent; losses can retry the current fight. Circuit progress is browser-local under `terminl:rumble-circuit:v1`.
- **Moon Mission:** `/os/moon` has a connected ten-level campaign. Routes are 13,000–26,000 world units, compared with the former 4,000–6,400. Levels allow five to eight minutes each. Routes demand timed jumps and increasingly dense hazard combinations; exploration and checkpoint retries add time.

## Racing visibility

Wen Lambo uses one projection for the road and both cars. The chase camera widens for nearby rivals so passes remain visible in portrait and landscape; both cars use the same depth ordering and stay visible above decorative scenery. Online rivals use the same predicted tick as the local car, rather than being rewound to an older snapshot.

A permanent checkerboard and finish sign mark gate zero, including after a finisher advances to its next checkpoint. The stripe spans the same lane bounds as the authoritative crossing test. Finishing rivals remain at their simulated crossing position. Scoring, steering, rules v5 and archived replays are unchanged.

`npm run test:race:visibility` renders 36 passing scenarios and six legal finish crossings in Chromium. The main arcade suite also checks both seats, all six tracks, narrow screens, finish coordinates and online prediction.

## Moon progression

| Level | World | Main challenge |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Degen District | Spike beds and patrols; learn the safe high route |
| 2 | Neon Rooftops | Stepped rooftops and telegraphed vent bursts |
| 3 | Emerald Overgrowth | Moving saws and hopping sentries |
| 4 | Diamond Caverns | Narrow crystal stairways above mixed traps |
| 5 | Sunset Dunes | Wider gaps, narrow bridges and crumbling high routes |
| 6 | Cold Storage | Slippery braking into denser trap combinations |
| 7 | Liquidation Foundry | Cannons with marked firing lanes, plus vents and saws |
| 8 | Cloud Nine | Narrow moving sky platforms and mixed traps |
| 9 | Orbital Shipyard | Dense cannon / saw / vent combinations and hopping guards |
| 10 | Tranquility Base | Four-trap gauntlets and the faster Liquidation Warden |

The final arena locks while the Warden lives. It telegraphs a charge, releases ground shockwaves, then exposes blue armor. Stomp its head during that opening. Six hits defeat it. Its blue opening lasts 90 frames (formerly 150), charges are faster, and each cycle fires one, two, then three pairs of shockwaves as health falls. The rocket cannot complete the campaign before the boss is defeated.

Every level increases hazards per world unit, from 14 spike beds in level 1 to 92 mixed traps in level 10. Upper ledges narrow from 150 to 105 units; gap bridges narrow from 80 to 55. Enemy patrols quicken and widen, additional guards enter from level 4, and shields become less frequent and require the high route. Vents and cannons always warn before firing; spikes and saws stay dangerous even when approached from above. Checkpoint positions and the save format are unchanged, with clear space around each respawn.

New levels unlock sequentially. Cleared levels can be replayed. Checkpoints save the current level and safe spawn, including the boss entrance. Retrying restores three health; previously passed pickups are removed and the attempt's score starts fresh. Saves use `terminl:moon-campaign:v1`; the old OS profile is left intact. Storage failure is reported and play continues for the current visit.

Keyboard, touch and controller input are supported. Blur, hidden tabs and pause release input. Portrait and landscape layouts keep controls outside the playfield.

## Verification

- `npm run test:os`: legacy replay coverage and the new campaign's traversal, boss, save, unlock, mechanic and render checks.
- `npm run test:arcade`: racing, combat, input, network and six-fight circuit checks.
- `npm run test:arcade:v1`: browser checks for flagship order, removed clutter, mobile controls, pause, circuit resume and all ten campaign wins through ordinary keyboard inputs. An accelerated animation clock samples rendering while leaving simulation state and save data untouched.
- `npm run test:rumble:circuit:browser`: wins all six circuit fights through keyboard input and verifies saved advancement and the mirror finale.
- `node scripts/arcade-visibility.mjs`: default production launcher and fighting-game navigation; asset tools stay gated.
